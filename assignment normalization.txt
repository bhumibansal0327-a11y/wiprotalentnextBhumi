Current Normal Form: 2NF

Reason: DNAME and LOC depend on DEPTNO, not directly on EMPNO.

EMPLOYEE - EMPNO - ENAME - SAL - DEPTNO

DEPARTMENT - DEPTNO - DNAME - LOC

Question 2

STUDENT - ROLLNO - NAME - AGE - EXAM - MARKS

GRADE - MARKS - GRADE

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Question 3

Problem: Composite key is (EMPNO, PROJECT_NO). CUSTOMERNAME depends only
on PROJECT_NO, so there is a partial dependency.

Current Normal Form: 1NF

Convert to 2NF:

EMPLOYEE_PROJECT - EMPNO - PROJECT_NO - NO_OF_DAYS

PROJECT - PROJECT_NO - CUSTOMERNAME

This removes repeated customer names.
